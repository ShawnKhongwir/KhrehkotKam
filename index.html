About this bank

Every question follows the topics and style of past MPSC Group C, Assistant and MCS papers and UPSC-style GK. They are original practice questions, labelled Practice (PYQ pattern). None is presented as a verbatim past-paper question, because official MPSC answer keys for most papers aren't published online.

Maths, Reasoning and number-system questions are generated from formula templates, and every answer is computed, not typed, so the arithmetic is exact. English, GK and Computer questions are written from standard facts. GK is static, with a few settled events up to 2025. Add the last 12 months of current affairs from a monthly compilation before your exam.

To solve real past papers alongside this bank, use the downloads on mpsc.meghalaya.gov.in and the collections on Adda247. UPSC Prelims papers are on upsc.gov.in.
