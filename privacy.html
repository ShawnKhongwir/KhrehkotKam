Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

What this site does

MPSC Question Bank is a free practice site for Meghalaya Public Service Commission exams. You can use it without an account.

Information we collect

Without signing in: your answers, wrong answers and marked questions are stored only in your own browser (local storage). They never reach our servers.

If you sign in with Google: we receive your name, email address and a user ID from Google through Firebase Authentication. We store your practice progress (which questions you answered, whether they were right, and which you marked) in Google Cloud Firestore, linked to that user ID. We do not collect passwords.

How long we keep it

Saved progress is deleted automatically 30 days after your last activity. Your sign-in record is kept by Firebase until you ask us to delete it. When you sign out, progress is also removed from the device you were using.

Advertising and cookies

This site shows ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads at Google Ads Settings, and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by Google (Firebase and AdSense) as our service providers under their own privacy policies.

Children

This site is intended for exam candidates aged 18 and over.

Your choices

You can use the site without signing in, sign out at any time, and ask us to delete your account data by writing to your-email@example.com.
